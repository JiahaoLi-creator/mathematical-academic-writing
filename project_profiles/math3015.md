## Teaching role

The notebook is a concise, beginner-facing visual companion for classroom explanation. Formal statements provide enough context to read the visual; figures, numerical experiments, and interpretations carry the explanatory work. Reproduce lecture proofs only when requested.

Short chapters finish after their final substantive section. Omit report scaffolding such as an Executive Summary, repeated Key Takeaway, or a conclusion that restates the notebook.

## Figure prose

- Center images and captions.
- Justify body text and interpretation paragraphs.
- Put the interpretation on a separate line below the caption.
- Use one concise paragraph when the figure is simple.
- Preserve longer beginner-facing explanations when several panels or scales form one argument.
- Explain unfamiliar parameters directly, such as stake rules, `n=32`, a sup-norm correction, or the constant in a scaling reference.
