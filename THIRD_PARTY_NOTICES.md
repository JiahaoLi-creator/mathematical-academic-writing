## anti-defensive-writing

[anti-defensive-writing](https://github.com/Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing) project, including
functional classification, positive-scope revision, precision preservation, and a review-first
workflow. The mathematical rules, examples, and validation design in this repository were
developed independently.
